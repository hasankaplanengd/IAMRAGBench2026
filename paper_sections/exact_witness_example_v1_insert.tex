\paragraph{Concrete authorization witness.}
Table~\ref{tab:exact-witness-example} shows request-level evidence
for one DynamoDB ABAC tenant-escape candidate. The trusted envelope
requires the generated policy to preserve the
\texttt{dynamodb:LeadingKeys} tenant boundary for
\texttt{tenant\#alpha}. For \texttt{dynamodb:Query}, the clean policy
allows the intended \texttt{tenant\#alpha} request and rejects the
forbidden \texttt{tenant\#beta} request. The poisoned policy preserves
the intended request but also authorizes \texttt{tenant\#beta}. This is
therefore counted as an exact authorization delta rather than merely as
textual drift or JSON-level difference.
